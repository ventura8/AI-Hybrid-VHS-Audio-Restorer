# v1.3.3

## Highlights

`auto` now chooses between `auto_pure_linear` and `cathar` on the evidence of
the real-tape corpus, and its acoustic profile block prints every measure the
choice rests on. Both engines were then tuned on real tapes by a harness that
scores an output the way a listener would, and ship its plateaus.

- `auto` never ran the two engines this project measures. Its three former
  engines leave the noise on the tape (0.11, 0.35 and 0.72 dB of noise removed
  over 136 clips, against 10.10 for `auto_pure_linear` and 6.02 for `cathar`).
- `auto` is the default. Every acoustic class runs `auto_pure_linear`, which
  leads `cathar` on each one, on the corpus and on 81 excerpts of 21 local
  tapes; `cathar` runs on sustained tonal programme with no silence for the
  noise probe -- the one condition, of some thirty readings scored on both sets,
  under which it deviates less on most clips, for 2-3 dB less removal
  (`auto_cathar_tonal`) -- and where the neural denoiser is not installed (when
  cathar is installed; with neither engine available the `auto_ffmpeg_native`
  chain is the last resort).
- The profile block adds rhythm, spectral flatness (the chain's own tonal
  gate), the mains notch, the material class, the verdict with its numbers,
  and both engines with whether each is installed.
- `enable_multipass` is no longer read; `multipass_auto` stays one
  `process_mode` away.
- A listener-like harness (gap air, pause depth, the 's', attacks, clicks,
  Whisper, speaker similarity; learned predictors as guardrails) and a
  self-driving loop that tunes each engine until no candidate qualifies.
- cathar's de-esser fault (a single-band threshold on the multiband stage:
  4-12 kHz down 30 dB, "under water" speech) and its 0.75 s noise probe are
  fixed; the print is stitched from six pauses on tapes of 90 s or more.
- The listener round's defaults: `cathar` at alpha 1.0, beta 0.02, repair 2,
  coherent and enhance off, de-esser 12 dB; `auto_pure_linear` on the
  Mel-RoFormer denoiser without the subtraction stage, sibilant guard 0.8,
  expander 12 dB; both with the pause floor on, the expander knee +8 dB and a
  20 LU loudness range; music-profile keys where music disagreed
  (`cathar_music_*`, `apl_music_neural_model`). The installers provision
  cathar 0.7.6.

## Verification

Every engine `auto` could dispatch measured on the same 136 clips with the
same trade metric; hardware validation of the new dispatch on the RTX 5090
(`auto` mid fixture 6.01x, 18,715 MB); `cathar` 5 of 5 bit-identical to the
identity reference re-based on the new defaults; the four tuning loops ran
to their plateaus on the full Tata tapes and 12 music clips; every gate
green.

## Upgrade Notes

`auto` is the default `process_mode`; an existing `config.yaml` keeps the
mode it names. `auto` output comes from the `auto_pure_linear` chain under
the `*_Auto_Cleaned` name (or `cathar` on tonal programme without silence);
set `process_mode` to `multipass_auto` to keep the earlier dialogue
behaviour, `auto_cathar_tonal` to false to keep `auto_pure_linear` on tonal
programme. Both engines ship the listener round's defaults (`cathar` output
is not bit-identical to v1.3.2); every moved key carries its evidence in
`config.yaml`. The installers provision cathar 0.7.6.
