# v1.3.3

## Highlights

`auto` now chooses between the two full restoration chains, `auto_pure_linear`
and `cathar`, on the evidence of the real-tape corpus, and its
`[AI Auto-Scanner] Acoustic Profile Analysis` block prints every measure that
choice rests on. The engines themselves are unchanged: `cathar` is
bit-identical to the v1.3.2 build on all five reference clips, and
`auto_pure_linear` output is untouched.

- **`auto` never ran the two engines this project measures.** It dispatched
  the four-pass stem-separation engine on dialogue, `denoise_only` on music
  and `auto_ffmpeg_native` on tape noise. Measured on the same 136 corpus
  clips with the same trade metric as the head-to-head benchmark (noise
  removed / programme deviation, dB, medians), those three leave the noise on
  the tape:

  | Engine | Dialogue (90) | Rhythmic music (45) | All (136) |
  | :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
  | `auto_pure_linear` | **10.10/0.29** | **11.63/0.36** | **10.10/0.31** |
  | `cathar` | 6.16/0.31 | 5.89/0.66 | 6.02/0.44 |
  | `multipass_auto` | -0.25/0.14 | 0.27/0.13 | 0.11/0.14 |
  | `denoise_only` | 0.33/0.13 | 0.73/0.23 | 0.35/0.16 |
  | `auto_ffmpeg_native` | 0.76/0.06 | 0.62/0.09 | 0.72/0.06 |

  The classes are the scanner's own, read on the same clips. The stem
  engine's generative enhancer adds level to the quiet frames of dialogue;
  the neural denoiser on its own, without the subtraction and repair stages
  around it, takes a third of a decibel.

- **`auto` is the default.** `auto_pure_linear` and `cathar` stay one line
  of `config.yaml` away.

- **What separates `cathar` from `auto_pure_linear`, measured on the corpus
  and on local tape.** `cathar` is beaten on both halves by
  `auto_pure_linear` on every class of the 136 corpus clips (70 clips to 8)
  and on 81 sixty-second excerpts of 21 local captures (10.12/0.07 against
  6.27/0.11; 44 to 4). Some thirty readings of the source were scored
  against the per-clip outcome on both sets, including readings built
  around the engines' mechanism -- `auto_pure_linear` learns its noise
  profile from the quietest 4 s at a factor tuned for speech, `cathar` from
  the quietest 0.75 s at a gentler one. No reading routes `cathar` to a
  class it wins outright; a leave-one-out search for one routes 16 clips and
  loses 9. The one condition both sets agree on is the mechanism itself:
  sustained tonal programme with no silence, where the quietest 4 s is
  shaped like the loud frames and the subtraction shaves the programme.
  There `cathar` deviates less on 10 of 14 corpus clips (0.21 dB against
  0.54) and 9 of 15 local excerpts (0.15 against 0.19), each time for 2-3 dB
  less noise removed.

- **`auto_pure_linear` on every class, `cathar` on that condition and where
  it is the best that can run.** The scanner reads the condition as spectral
  flatness under `auto_cathar_flatness_max` (0.04), the quietest 4 s shaped
  like the loud frames above `auto_cathar_probe_similarity` (0.9), and no
  sustained beat, and hands it to `cathar` as a fidelity preference
  (`auto_cathar_tonal`, default true). `cathar`, the deterministic engine
  that needs no model, also runs wherever the neural denoiser is not
  installed; `auto_ffmpeg_native` remains the last resort when neither is.
  Routed this way the corpus reads 9.74/0.27 against 10.10/0.31 for
  `auto_pure_linear` throughout, the local excerpts 10.00/0.07 against
  10.12/0.07. The rule and its numbers sit together in
  `modules/auto_scanner.py` and in the benchmark document's new sections,
  "What `auto` runs" and "What separates `cathar` from `auto_pure_linear`".

- **The profile block reports what the choice rests on.** Beside the speech,
  music and ambient ratios and the noise floor, the scanner now prints the
  onset periodicity against the beat threshold, the spectral flatness
  against the chain's tonal gate (the same `estimate_tonality` measure the
  subtraction factor is gated on, so the log shows the number the engine
  will act on), what the noise probe would learn from, the mains notch, then
  the material class, the verdict with its corpus numbers, and both engines
  with whether each is installed.
  `auto_pure_linear` and `cathar` print the same block as advisory when they
  run directly.

- **`enable_multipass` is no longer read.** It only ever redirected `auto`'s
  dialogue class to `multipass_auto`; the four-pass engine is still one
  `process_mode` away.

## Verification

- Every engine `auto` could dispatch was measured on the 136 readable corpus
  clips at v1.3.2 with `scripts/measure_tradeoff.py`, and the 136 clips were
  profiled with the scanner itself so the classes in the table are the ones
  the shipped code reads. `cathar` and `auto_pure_linear` were then measured
  on 84 sixty-second excerpts of the 21 local captures (81 readable), and
  some thirty readings of the source were scored against the per-clip
  outcome on both sets (`experiments/auto_engines_v140/`) before the one
  condition the sets agree on was adopted.
- Hardware validation on the RTX 5090 with the new dispatch: `auto` restored
  both generated fixtures through the `auto_pure_linear` chain (short 2.28x,
  5,153 MB; mid 6.01x, 18,715 MB, against 2.16x and 19,255 MB through the
  stem engine in v1.3.2) and `cathar` passed beside it (1.06x / 1.18x, under
  2 GB).
- `cathar` identity: 5 of 5 reference clips decode bit-identically to the
  v1.3.2 outputs.
- Quality gate: 1164 tests, 95.34% total coverage, every file at
  or above 90%, Radon CC and MI grade A, ruff / black / isort / flake8 /
  bandit clean, Markdown gates clean.

## Upgrade Notes

`auto` is the default `process_mode`, so a fresh `config.yaml` restores through
the `auto_pure_linear` chain under the `*_Auto_Cleaned` name, or through
`cathar` on sustained tonal programme without silence and on a machine without
the neural stack (when cathar is installed; with neither engine available the
`auto_ffmpeg_native` chain is the last resort); an existing `config.yaml` keeps
whatever mode it names. To keep the earlier `auto` behaviour on dialogue, set
`process_mode` to `multipass_auto`; to keep `auto_pure_linear` on tonal
programme too, set `auto_cathar_tonal` to false. `enable_multipass` is accepted
and ignored.
