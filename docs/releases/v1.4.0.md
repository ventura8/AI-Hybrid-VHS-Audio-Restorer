# v1.4.0

## Highlights

Both engines take more tape hiss out of the programme and the pauses on
default settings, by changes the user chose in blind listening, and the
output-quality harness (the "AI human ear") that found them now reads hiss
under speech and in the pauses the way the user asked: very critically,
without rewarding dead air or a dulled programme.

- **Less hiss in `auto_pure_linear` (and `auto`).** The reference-SNR
  suppressor and a per-frame pause fill shelved at 2 kHz run on every file
  whose noise print the suppressor can trust. Blind against v1.3.8's
  settings on the four Tata tapes: preferred 9-0 on each of the three
  interview tapes, no difference on the choir (8 same, 1 for v1.3.8). 9 of
  202 Internet Archive (IA) hunt clips cross a hiss-ear guard; the
  realistic-v2 fixtures render unchanged.
- **Less hiss in `cathar`.** The same suppressor with a 6 dB programme
  release, the per-frame fill (cut 18 dB) and a 12 dB expander at
  0.08 / 0.12 s. Blind against v1.3.8's settings: 9-0 on each of the four
  Tata tapes. 6 of 235 IA clips cross a guard; realistic-v2 holds (LSD
  -0.01 dB, 1.1 dB more noise removed). Tuned again at the listener's
  calibrated level, cathar's own fill is 24 dB and its suppressor's bias
  1.5: it never lost a blind pick to the set before it (3-0, 33 same), 5 of
  235 IA clips cross a guard against it, realistic-v2 is unmoved.
- **Ear v4, critical of hiss.** Two readings of the hiss left under the
  programme and in the pauses over their masked thresholds, two flags fixed
  at 0 dB by the user's rule, and hard guards against dead pauses, gated
  hiss, musical noise and dulling.
- **Blind listening with a ledger.** `scripts/listen_ab.py` serves blind A/B
  blocks on 127.0.0.1 with catch trials, a fixed speech level and a recorded
  playback chain; every answer goes to the verdict ledger, and a threshold
  run sets the level the hiss readings assume for that listener.
- **Long speech tapes in `auto_pure_linear` no longer fail.** Since v1.3.3
  every speech tape longer than one neural chunk (14 min on a 16 GB machine)
  failed in `auto_pure_linear` and `auto`.
- **The restored track starts where the source's audio started**, and
  MPEG-PS keeps its source's sample rate.
- **Exit status 1 when any file fails or is skipped**, 2 when nothing ran.
- **Three security fixes**: a planted `config.yaml` could inject FFmpeg
  filters through `arnndn_model`, a folder of tapes could write through a
  planted batch log link, and a planted `.temp_work_<stem>` link let the
  resume sweep delete files in the tree it pointed to.
- **Dependencies**: the torch 2.14.1 CUDA 13.0 set deferred in v1.3.8,
  `poetry lock` in seconds instead of hours, cathar 0.8.0, FFmpeg 9.0.2 in
  the Windows installer.

## The reference-SNR suppressor and the pause floor

The user heard hiss on every Tata tape for weeks. The hiss diagnosis of
2026-10-11 found the causes in `auto_pure_linear`: the Mel-RoFormer is the
only stage that removes hiss and is weakest at 3-4.8 kHz, and the pause
floor keeper put the hiss back. The RoFormer takes the true pauses to about
-93 dB; the flat fill, aimed at the quiet cluster's median and summed on
the floor the neural stage passed, brought them back to -10.9 dB (SOTI) and
-4.1 dB (Tele7abc) against the source, -7.1 dB at the IA hunt's median.

The pause floor (`modules/pause_floor.py`, both engines) gains:

- `pause_floor_target`: `frame` aims each quiet frame `pause_floor_fill_db`
  under its own source frame, scaled by the restored programme's gain;
  `cluster` is v1.3.8's fill.
- `pause_floor_shape`: `shelf` (also `lowpass` and `hiss_shelf`) takes the
  fill from a zero-phase low-passed copy of the source, room tone under
  `pause_floor_shelf_hz` and the rest `pause_floor_hf_cut_db` down. Pause
  hiss against the source at the hunt's median: -7.6 dB (v1.3.8), -13.4
  (frame), -30.3 (frame and a 3 kHz shelf).
- `pause_floor_suppressed_only`: the configured fill only on a file the
  reference suppressor ran on; any other file keeps v1.3.8's fill (cluster,
  flat, 12 dB) and its bytes. The APL plateau's IA confirmation failed
  without it: 31 of 202 clips tripped a guard, and on the 135 clips whose
  print the suppressor refuses every trip was the per-frame fill gating
  pauses under a programme that keeps its hiss.

The reference-SNR suppressor (`modules/reference_suppress.py`, both
engines) reads the MMSE-LSA gain on the pre-conditioned source's SNR and
applies it to the restored audio. Its noise is the source's whole-file
quantile print (per bin, the 5th percentile of the 0.37 s moving mean,
+2 dB). The restoring stage's mask and the source's SNR multiply, so it only
removes and runs ungated. The existing post-neural suppressor could not do
this: its gate stays shut on the Tata tapes, and its noise, the quietest
4 s, read 13-25 dB over the true pauses below 8 kHz on all four (an
interview's quietest 4 s hold speech).

- A print whose loud frames stand less than 23 dB over it learned the
  programme, and the suppressor does not run. The line was set on 202 IA
  hunt clips: prints 18-23 dB down dulled the loud highs on 11 of 37 clips
  for no more hiss removed. The Tata tapes sit at 24.7-31.9 dB; every
  realistic-v2 fixture and 135 of the 202 IA clips sit under the line.
- The programme release (`apl_reference_release_db`,
  `cathar_reference_release_db`, 0 = off) eases the gain where the restored
  audio stands the release over the source's pause print, so programme the
  restoring stage kept is not cut twice. It reads the pause print (the
  median over the source's pause frames, never under the print) because
  Vaccin's MP3 coder drops 10-16 kHz in its speech frames, which pulled the
  5th-percentile print 11.7 dB under the hiss in its pauses. The APL loop
  refused the release at 3 dB and at 6 dB on real renders (Tele7abc's pause
  tail +0.62), so `auto_pure_linear` keeps it off; cathar takes 6 dB.
- On CPU replay with the fill shelved at 2 kHz by 30 dB, the hiss under
  speech over its masked threshold (at 75 dB SPL) fell to 0 on all four
  Tata tapes for both engines (APL from up to 9.43 dB, cathar from
  7.75-14.53 dB), every hiss-ear guard passing.

- Session 0 (2026-10-09, six blind blocks): the raw tape against the shipped

## Ear v4: critical of hiss

The user's rule (2026-10-11): the ear must be very critical of VHS hiss
under speech or singing and in silence and pauses, and "no hiss" never
means dead air or a damaged programme. The harness had not moved on weeks
of hiss verdicts: its hiss flag was display only and nothing read the hiss
under the programme.

- `dsp.hiss_prog_excess_db` reads the hiss left under the programme over
  its masked threshold, `dsp.hiss_pause_excess_db` the hiss-weighted pause
  floor over the threshold in quiet, each file's loud frames placed at a
  declared playback level (75 dB SPL unmeasured). The flags
  `listener.hiss_programme` and `listener.hiss_pause` fire at 0 dB, fixed
  by the user's rule: no calibration or gates file moves them.
- `tata_v4.yaml` and `music_v4.yaml` (the loop's and the sweep's default)
  rank the residual hiss one-sided with inaudible as the target, and hard
  guards keep a loop from winning by deleting programme: no new audible
  hiss, pause depth at most 35 dB with the room band kept, no hiss gated out
  of the pauses but kept under speech (`dsp.hiss_pump_db`, bound 12), no
  musical noise, no dulling of the loud or the soft programme, and DNSMOS
  BAK and SIGMOS NOISE as vetoes.
- Each hiss reading's tie is the widest same-percept transform's p95 (a
  fresh draw of the same noise, a 0.37-sample delay, +-0.1 dB, a soxr round
  trip, requantisation), re-derived on whole tapes: 0.40 and 0.45 under the
  programme, 0.35 in the pauses.
- Three validation rounds and an adversarial review fixed the readings at
  their cause: a perfect output read +9 to +22 dB (now inaudible on 41 of 43
  linear fixtures); a 30-50 dB gate of the pauses passed the pump guard
  through the frames straddling its edges; a bounded guard could creep one
  step per accepted round (11, 14, 17: dead air in three rounds); a
  hiss-free room floor under 2 kHz read +9.7 dB of pause hiss (the reading
  now weighs hiss half at 3.15 kHz); a choir's soft singing read as pause
  hiss (pause runs whose source holds partials are left out: the pause tail
  of Gaudeamus5's APL per-frame fill render 21.67 to 13.14, the speech
  tapes unchanged).
- Later guards: the soft programme 6-18 dB over the hiss
  (`dsp.hiss_prog_kept_soft_db`, bound -27 dB); an inaudible residual reads
  no islands; the dulling guard no longer reads a coded source's hiss as
  loud programme (APL's IA material trips 14 to 9 on the 64 clips the
  suppressor runs on).
- Not reached: dense full-band singing. A second, stationary anchor
  validates no real source, and neither a band edge, a split in time nor
  the within-band valleys tells revealed programme from residual hiss
  (`docs/ear_v4_hiss.md`).

## Listening and the listener's level

- `listen_ab` (2026-10-12) switches stimuli at the same playhead, plays
  every cut at -23 dBFS on its speech frames, opens with practice, scores
  interleaved catch trials apart, offers "can't tell", requires `--device`
  and `--volume`, and flags a one-button block "no signal" (only a missed
  catch reads "disengaged"). Its threshold mode runs 3-interval tone
  detection at 1-12.5 kHz, now forced choice: the first run answered "can't
  tell" on 45 of 60 trials, which left each frequency's band about 45 dB
  wide.

  its top end: it was caught only on Tele7abc cuts that still carried it.
- With `AI_RESTORE_LISTENER` naming a calibrated listener, the audibility
  check floors its masked threshold on that listener's threshold per
  frequency, and the hiss readings stand on the threshold run's loud-frame
  level plus 4 dB: 86.8 dB SPL for wired headphones at Windows 70 %.
  Session 2's listener preferred cathar's final over the lighter set 21-0
  while the readings at 75 dB SPL read both as inaudible; re-read, the 17
  blind verdicts at that setup reproduce 13 at 75, 15 at 84 and 16 at 87.
  The loop's score files carry the record's id. Unset, nothing changes.
- Session 3 (2026-10-29) heard cathar's calibrated-level plateau against
  the set before it: 3-0 with 33 same, and hiss in the pauses on both, as
  the readings say (6-19 and 9-21 dB over the threshold in quiet). About

- `docs/ai_human_ear_architecture.md` describes the ear in eight layers,

## Alignment and the time base

- Every paired reading now aligns by GCC-PHAT on the source's loudest spans
  voting (`scripts/pair_alignment.py`), with abstention and polarity. On the
  IA hunt it reads the true lag within 8 samples on 203 cathar and 202 APL
  outputs of 205 each, against the old head search's 137 and 199, and all
  eight shipped Tata outputs exactly (the head search missed five by 6-181
  samples). Scores stored before 2026-10-11 need re-scoring.
- R12 reads the time base: DTW holds, 40 ms plateaus, rate jumps and speed.
  The app's DTW plays whole 40 ms frames at 0x or 2x: 177 holds on 24 of 30
  cathar and 49 on 15 of 30 APL hunt outputs sent to DTW, none on the 205
  per engine sent to shift.
- `sync_dtw_enable: false` sends the length-preserving chains
  (`auto_pure_linear`, cathar, the single-track modes) to shift sync; on the
  GPU it took 26 hunt DTW clips' holds from 42 to 0 (APL) and 154 to 0
  (cathar) with the sync held. Default true until a blind block. The CPU DTW
  path falls back to shift today (a `multiprocessing.Event` that cannot be
  pickled); documented, not fixed.
- `azimuth_estimate_method: spans` reads the azimuth skew from a GCC-PHAT
  vote with the sign the graph corrects. The default head reading has the
  opposite sign: on the 43 hunt clips it delayed it doubled the skew and
  lowered the mono 4-8 kHz on 40 (median -1.43 dB); `spans` delayed 28 and
  raised it on 27 (median +0.45 dB). The default stays `head` until heard.

## The self-driving tuning loop

- Judging (`scripts/autotune_judging.py`): a tie band per reading from the
  unheard Session 0 pairs, hiss and noise readings on scorer jitter only, a
  family-wise veto (its null rate 0-1.5 % against 44-68 % for the any-tape
  rule), a tape win only on a move past the band the better way, and
  bounded guards that do not follow the incumbent past their bound.
- Cost: only the families the grid reads (the stems separation took about
  74 of the 113 minutes SOTI's scoring ran in a speech round), the learned
  judges only on the incumbent and the contenders, music-only knobs left
  out on speech tapes, and the stage cache (`AI_RESTORE_STAGE_CACHE`,
  `--stage-cache`), which replays the neural stage when only later settings
  change: SOTI in 27.7 s on a hit against 61.4 s fresh.
- `scripts/rejudge_autotune.py` judges stored rounds again offline;
  `scripts/variation_precheck.py` found no per-tape choice above the unheard
  control except APL's stem path on three music clips, so the GRPO settings
  policy stays parked; joint moves propose pairs one knob cannot reach;
  `scripts/confirm_trips.py` tabulates a plateau's corpus confirmation.
- A failed render is set aside and the run stops on the incumbent's
  failure, three failures in a row or under 40 GB free; an output already
  beside a tape is moved aside before a render; tuning batches stay under
  Windows' command-line limit. Never two restorations on one source path.
- The hiss, scan and noise-print keys of this release are loop knobs.
- `--freeze KNOB` holds a knob a corpus confirmation refused, and the corpus
  guard (`--corpus` sentinel IA clips, `--corpus-max-trips`) asks a round's
  qualifying candidates best first and accepts the first whose render on the
  sentinels trips at most that many clips against the run's start settings:
  three plateaus in a row had failed their IA confirmation after the loop
  accepted them (31 of 202, 57 of 235, 31 of 235).

## Engine fixes

- **Long speech tapes in `auto_pure_linear`.** The chunked denoise accepted
  only a `(No Noise)` stem; the Mel-RoFormer writes `(dry)` and `(other)`.
  One clean-stem rule (`modules/denoise_model.py`) and an MDXC chunk grid
  with the whole file's input gain fix it; the model's files are fetched
  before the chunks are cut. On the GPU 99.67 % of the chunked render's
  samples equal the whole-file render, seams within -150.5 dBFS, 2.3-2.4 GB
  peak.
- **Mux timing and rate** (`modules/mux_format.py`). The restored track
  used to start with the video: on synthetic sources with the audio 300 ms
  late a click landed 273.7 ms early in MP4 and MOV, 295.0 ms in MKV and
  285.0 ms in MPEG-PS. It now starts where the source's audio started in
  every container tried; MPEG-PS and MPEG-TS keep their timestamp-jump
  correction. MPEG-PS keeps its source's rate (44.1 kHz before, which
  DVD-Video does not allow for MP2). `mux_sample_rate` (`pipeline`) and
  `mux_limiter_latency_compensation` (false; the limiter delays every
  output 219 samples, 4.97 ms at 44.1 kHz) are new.
- With `preserve_original_audio_track`, the restored track is the default
  one (MP4 and MOV used to open on the archival copy).
- Child processes are decoded as UTF-8 with replacement: a Cyrillic title
  had dropped the mux to single-pass loudness normalisation.
- A source with no audio stream, or one that decodes to no samples, is a
  logged `[Skip]`; an ffmpeg failure no retry changes is raised at once
  instead of retried six times on fewer threads, while a memory message or
  a damaged capture's decode warnings keep the retry.
- Exit status 0 when every file was restored, 1 when any failed or was
  skipped, 2 when nothing ran; "Press Enter to exit..." only on a console
  (on Windows a detached stdin reported a tty).
- The loudness log applies ffmpeg's whole linear-mode rule: 37 % of the
  logged "linear" decisions had run dynamic.
- Stage output names are bounded at 120 characters (a Tata tape's sync
  output reached 261, past NTFS's 255). Fallbacks log at WARNING. The
  RNNoise models are binary to Git, so a Windows checkout keeps the bytes
  their SHA-256 pins name.

## Security

- **FFmpeg filter injection through `arnndn_model`.** The value reached a
  single-quoted option of the `arnndn_speech` filter graph unsanitised, and
  a quote in it let a planted `config.yaml` inject filters that open
  attacker-chosen local files; a `models/` folder in the launch directory,
  or a link in it, also supplied the model. Now `arnndn_model` must be a
  bare `.rnnn` filename, is looked up only in the application's own model
  store, a store file that links out of it is skipped, and filter-graph
  metacharacters in a path are refused. The default graph is unchanged.
- **Batch logs through a planted link.** With `batch_jobs` over 1 (the
  shipped `config.yaml` sets 8) each child's output went to
  `logs/<stem>.log` in the launch directory, opened following symlinks,
  and a file's name printed with its line breaks: a folder of tapes with
  that log linked to `~/.bashrc` and a crafted video name could write a
  payload line there. The logs now live beside the application, open
  refusing a link, and names reach logs and the console on one line.
- **The work directory through a planted link.** Each file's work
  directory, `.temp_work_<stem>`, sits beside the tape. A folder of tapes
  that shipped it as a link to the user's home had it accepted (the mkdir
  followed the link): the resume sweep then unlinked every `*.tmp`,
  `*.tmp.*` and hidden `*.wav` file in the tree it points to, and the stages
  wrote their intermediates there. The work directory is now refused when it
  is a symbolic link, a Windows junction or not a directory, and the file is
  skipped; the sweep follows no link and removes every link inside a real
  work directory (one named `original.tmp.wav` had been left for the
  extraction's `ffmpeg -y` to write through); the cleanup never removes
  through a link; a stale partial that is a dangling link is removed
  instead of written through.

## Upgrade Notes

`auto_pure_linear`, `cathar` and `auto` sound different on default
settings: less hiss under speech and in the pauses, because the user
preferred it blind on the four Tata tapes and the IA and realistic-v2
confirmations held.

- `auto_pure_linear`: `apl_reference_suppress: true`,
  `pause_floor_target: frame`, `pause_floor_shape: shelf`,
  `pause_floor_shelf_hz: 2000`, `pause_floor_hf_cut_db: 12`,
  `pause_floor_fill_db: 18` (was 12) and
  `pause_floor_suppressed_only: true`. A file whose print sits within 23 dB
  of its loud frames keeps v1.3.8's chain. With
  `apl_reference_suppress: false` no file runs the suppressor, and every
  file takes v1.3.8's pause fill.
- `cathar`: `cathar_reference_suppress: true`,
  `cathar_reference_release_db: 6.0`, `cathar_suppress_noise_bias: 1.5`,

  `cathar_expander_depth_db: 12.0`, `cathar_expander_attack_s: 0.08`,
  `cathar_expander_decay_s: 0.12`; the shared `pause_floor_target`,
  `pause_floor_shape`, `pause_floor_shelf_hz` and
  `pause_floor_suppressed_only` shape cathar's fill too. A file whose print
  the suppressor refuses keeps cathar's subtraction and v1.3.8's fill but
  takes the new expander. `cathar_reference_suppress: false` and the three
  `cathar_expander_*` keys at 7.0 dB, 0.04 s and 0.18 s give cathar's
  earlier suppression and polish (the fill and bias keys act only on
  suppressed files).

  the video, so a source whose audio did not start with its video comes

  `mux_sample_rate: pipeline` an MPEG-PS output keeps its source's sample
  rate (48 kHz MP2 stays 48 kHz; a rate MP2 cannot encode becomes 48 kHz)
