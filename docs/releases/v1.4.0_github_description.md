# v1.4.0

## Highlights

- **Less hiss in `auto_pure_linear` and `cathar` by default.** A
  reference-SNR suppressor and a per-frame pause fill shelved at 2 kHz take
  the hiss out from under speech and out of the pauses. Heard blind on four
  real tapes: `auto_pure_linear` preferred 27-0 over v1.3.8 on the three
  interview tapes (no difference on the choir), `cathar` 36-0 on all four.
  On the Internet Archive set 9 of 202 and 6 of 235 clips cross a guard; the
  realistic-v2 fixtures render unchanged (APL) or hold (cathar). Tuned again
  at the listener's measured level, cathar's own pause fill is 24 dB and its
  suppressor's bias 1.5: never a blind pick lost (3-0, 33 same), 5 of 235
  clips crossing a guard against the set before it.

- **The harness is critical of hiss.** Ear v4 reads the hiss left under the
  programme and in the pauses over their masked thresholds, at the playback
  level a listener's threshold run measured, with guards against dead
  pauses, gated hiss and dulling. A blind A/B tool records every answer in
  a verdict ledger.

- **Long speech tapes in `auto_pure_linear` no longer fail.** Since v1.3.3
  every speech tape longer than one neural chunk (14 min on a 16 GB machine)
  failed.

- **A/V placement and exit status.** The restored track starts where the
  source's audio started, MPEG-PS keeps its source's sample rate, and the
  app exits 1 when any file fails or is skipped.

- **Three security fixes**: FFmpeg filter injection through `arnndn_model`
  from a planted `config.yaml`, batch logs written through a planted link
  in the launch folder, and a planted `.temp_work_<stem>` link that let the
  resume sweep delete files where it pointed.

- torch 2.14.1 (CUDA 13.0), `poetry lock` in seconds, cathar 0.8.0, FFmpeg
  9.0.2 in the Windows installer.

## Upgrade Notes

`auto_pure_linear`, `cathar` and `auto` output changes on default settings:
less hiss under speech and in the pauses. `apl_reference_suppress: false`
turns the new stage off in `auto_pure_linear` (every file then takes
v1.3.8's pause fill); `cathar_reference_suppress: false` with
`cathar_expander_depth_db: 7.0`, `cathar_expander_attack_s: 0.04` and
`cathar_expander_decay_s: 0.18` gives cathar's earlier suppression and
polish. cathar now has its own `cathar_pause_floor_fill_db` (24) and
`cathar_suppress_noise_bias` (1.5). A `config.yaml` in the launch folder
overrides the new defaults key by key: one kept from v1.3.8 sets
`pause_floor_fill_db: 12.0` for `auto_pure_linear`, where the heard setting
is 18. The restored track sits where the source's audio started against the
video (it used to start with the video), and an MPEG-PS output keeps its
source's sample rate. `arnndn_model` must be a bare `.rnnn` filename in the
application's model folder (anything else falls back to `cb.rnnn`), and
batch logs go to `logs/` beside the application.
