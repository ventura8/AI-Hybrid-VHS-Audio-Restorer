## torch 2.15.0 on cu132

The cu130 index ends at torch 2.14.1. torch 2.15.0 (2026-10-28) comes with a
matched torchvision 0.29.2 and torchaudio 2.11.1 on cu132 only, so the
source moves to `pytorch-cu132`, and `nvidia-cublas` 13.4.1.3 and
`nvidia-cuda-nvrtc` 13.2.86 follow its cuda-toolkit 13.2.2. The RTX 5090's
driver (621.01) supports CUDA up to 13.5.

It renders `auto_pure_linear` the same way on every run, but not as 2.14.1
did: the neural stage's numerics changed. `cathar` never loads torch and is
unchanged. Rendered at the shipped settings and judged against the shipped
renders:

- IA hunt, 235 clips: 0 material trips of the v4 guards; 16 strict trips,
  on clips already past a bound, each moving 0.06 dB or less.
- realistic-v2, 92 fixtures: 0 further from their targets and 0 closer;
  log-spectral distance and residual noise +0.00 dB in every class.
- Tata tapes: hiss under the programme and in the pauses, pumping, kept
  highs and noise removed each within 0.08 dB (most within 0.005); 0 guard
  trips.
- Tata audibility: Tele7abc, SOTI and Vaccin inaudible (their null 73 to
  80 dB down); Gaudeamus5 flagged.

The Gaudeamus5 flag is single frames where 4-10 kHz differs by 12-15 dB SPL
inside 72-84 dB SPL choir frames, against a masked threshold the model puts
at 1-4 dB SPL there, while every ranked reading of the tape stays within
0.08 dB. The check is one-sided ("audible" is not a verdict), and session 3
heard nothing in pairs it flagged. The comparison is clean: eight IA clips
rendered with 2.14.1 on this release's code matched the stored baseline
sample for sample, and the Tata tapes were rendered fresh with both torch
versions.

The `auto_pure_linear` identity reference is re-based on torch 2.15.0.

## The ear decides

`AGENTS.md` ("Judging Outputs: the AI Human Ear"), the output-quality-harness
skill ("Confirming a change with the ear alone"), the tuning workflow and
`docs/ai_human_ear_architecture.md` carry the rule. Every stage or setting
the documents said waits for a blind listening session now waits for the
ear's confirmation. The verdict ledger keeps sessions 0-3 as the ear's
calibration, and `scripts/listen_ab.py` stays for the record; nothing starts
it for the user.
